# Lesson 1 (synthetic sample)

## Dialogue

A: Konnichiwa. Ogenki desu ka.
B: Hai, genki desu. Anata wa?

## Vocabulary

| Japanese | Reading | English |
|---|---|---|
| konnichiwa | konnichiwa | hello |
| genki | genki | fine / energetic |

## Grammar Point

The particle wa marks the topic of the sentence.

## Exercise

1. Translate: "I am fine, thank you."
2. Fill in the blank: watashi __ genki desu.
